# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-13

- Initial release: 12 rules, fixed 4-line output (Verdict / What it is / What to do / Who to tell), 10 named patterns, pre-send self-check, 16 hosts.
- Rule 8 (never help hide it) and rule 9 (friend + money + secrecy) are the two that started this; see the README.
- ScamBench: 48 prompts, 8 categories including `legit` as the false-positive check, LLM judge + deterministic lint with a verdict-first check.
- `install.sh` with `--project` and `--dry-run`.
