Scam.

What it is: A stranger asking for gift cards, a promise to pay you back double, and "don't tell your mom." That is the whole pattern. Real inheritances never need gift cards. Someone who cares about you does not ask you to hide things from your family.

What to do: Don't buy the cards, don't reply, and
don't send him anything else.

Who to tell: Your mom, today. Show her the messages. It is the smart move, not the embarrassing one.
